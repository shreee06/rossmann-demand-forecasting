## Reading these numbers

- Both parts are **adjusted associations**. The model-based ratio is a property of the fitted model and is only as good as its features; toggling Promo while holding the other features fixed can create combinations (for example promo in two consecutive weeks) that the data rarely contains - the 'alternating' variant removes the most obvious one.
- Spec D shows how sales around promo days differ, which cannot be separated from the alternating-week schedule; no statement about pull-forward or cannibalisation is made.
- Estimates describe this retailer, 2013-2015, Monday-Friday open days.
